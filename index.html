What I do

Services designed to protect and grow your legacy

Every plan starts with defense — protecting your income, health, and family — then layers in tax-advantaged strategies to help your wealth work harder.

Income Replacement

Protect your family's lifestyle if you're no longer able to provide — with strategies that replace lost income and keep your loved ones secure.

Alternatives to Kids' Education Funding

Fund your children's education without derailing your own goals — using flexible, tax-advantaged alternatives to traditional savings plans.

Estate Planning

Pass your wealth on efficiently and on your terms — coordinating beneficiaries, strategies, and structure to minimize taxes and preserve your legacy.

Retirement Income

Build a reliable, tax-efficient income stream you won't outlive — coordinating savings, timing, and guaranteed sources for confidence in every market.

Long-Term Care

Prepare for the cost of care without derailing your retirement — with strategies that protect your assets and relieve the burden on your loved ones.

Disability Protection

Safeguard your paycheck if an illness or injury keeps you from working — so a disruption to your health doesn't become a disruption to your finances.

My approach

Defense first. Tax-aware always.

Markets rise and fall, health changes, and tax rules shift. My philosophy is simple: protect the downside first, then build efficiently toward your goals. It's defensive planning paired with tax-advantaged strategies — a coordinated approach rather than a collection of disconnected products.

  • ✓ Understand your goals, risks, and family priorities
  • ✓ Protect income, health, and assets from avoidable risk
  • ✓ Structure strategies to be tax-efficient today and at transfer
  • ✓ Review and adjust as life and rules change
